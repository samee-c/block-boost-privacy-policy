Privacy Policy

Game: Block Boost
Last Updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Boost ("the Game"), developed by SuperJi ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use our mobile game.

We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about the information that may be processed while you use Block Boost.

1. About the Game

Block Boost is an offline puzzle game designed for general audiences. The core gameplay can be played without an internet connection.

2. Information Collection

We do not operate our own account system and do not directly collect personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address through the gameplay.

However, the third-party advertising service used in the Game may automatically process certain information to provide, measure, and improve advertisements.

This information may include:

  • Device information and device identifiers
  • Advertising ID, where available and permitted
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information
  • Information used for fraud prevention and ad measurement

3. Advertising

Block Boost uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner ads and interstitial ads.

Google AdMob may collect and process information in accordance with its own privacy policies and terms.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit:

Google Privacy Policy

You can learn more about Google's advertising practices here:

How Google uses information from apps that use its services

4. Internet Connection

The main gameplay of Block Boost works offline. An internet connection may be required to load and display advertisements or to access certain advertising-related services.

5. Local Game Data

Block Boost may store certain gameplay information, such as your high score or game progress, locally on your device.

This information is used to provide gameplay features. We do not operate a server to collect or store this gameplay data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by Google AdMob and its service providers as necessary to provide advertising, measurement, security, and fraud prevention services.

7. Children's Privacy

Block Boost is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Advertising services may process information as described in their own privacy policies and applicable settings.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the Game. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services, including Google AdMob. These services may have their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage users to review the relevant policies.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You may be able to manage advertising preferences and advertising identifiers through your device settings. The availability of these controls may vary by device and operating system.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
